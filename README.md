# History of Halepete

## Introduction

Halepete was a village located in N.R. Pura Taluk, Karnataka. The village was submerged due to the formation of the Bhadra Reservoir backwaters.

## Relocation

After submergence, residents relocated to various places including:

- Aldara Village (Byrapura-Muttinakoppa Gram Panchayat)
- Muttinakoppa
- N.R. Pura
- Shivamogga
- Nearby settlements

Many families had to rebuild their lives after leaving their ancestral homes.

## Remains of the Village

Even today, during summer when water levels recede, remnants of the old village can be seen, including:

- Old roads
- Bridges
- Temple ruins
- House remains

## Current Challenges

Many displaced families continue to face challenges such as:

- Water scarcity during summer
- Forest animal conflicts
- Environmental concerns including Kasturirangan recommendations

## Community Memory

Halepete continues to live in the memories of former residents and their descendants.
